Vetflicks Waiting Room

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/11/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OneContact SL, operator of the Vetflicks Waiting Room application (the "App"), processes personal data. We are the data controller. You can contact us at info@onecontact.se or Calle Padilla 323, Ent 67c, 08025 Barcelona, Spain.

1. What we process

The App runs on screens installed at veterinary clinics and plays video content in a loop. To operate it we process:

  • Account data of the clinic operator who logs in (email address / username, authentication tokens managed by Amazon Cognito).
  • Device and diagnostic data (app version, connectivity status, playback logs) used to keep the service running and to provide support.
  • Subscription status associated with the clinic account.

The App is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect data from, patients or the general public viewing the screen.

2. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Providing the service and authenticating access — performance of a contract.
  • Security, diagnostics and fraud prevention — legitimate interests.
  • Complying with legal obligations — legal obligation.

3. Who we share with

We use service providers acting as processors, including Amazon Web Services (hosting, Cognito authentication, CloudFront content delivery). Data is stored in the European Union (AWS region Stockholm, Sweden). We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Retention

We keep account and subscription data for the life of the account and for 12 months afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law. Diagnostic logs are kept for 90 days.

5. Your rights

Subject to GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing. To exercise these, or to request the deletion of your account, contact info@onecontact.se. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (e.g. AEPD in Spain or IMY in Sweden).

6. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and access control to our systems.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the App.
